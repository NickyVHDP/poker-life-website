Buying the books

Orders & Checkout

How do I buy a Poker Life book?

Open the books page, use “View details” to learn more, then add the title to your cart. The cart icon at the top of the page takes you to one shared order review for all selected books.

What does a book cost?

The listed price appears on each book card and in the cart. Shipping, taxes, and any applicable fees are confirmed during secure Stripe checkout.

What format is the book — print, ebook, or audio?

Each book’s Poker Life details view includes its current overview. If anything is not clear after you have looked, ask through the contact page before buying.

How and when will I receive my order?

Delivery and timing are not published on this site, and quoting a timeframe that has not been committed to would not be fair to you. What happens after payment is set out by the product and checkout details on the Stripe page.

If you need to confirm delivery before ordering, ask first through the contact page.

Is checkout secure? What happens to my card details?

The cart is handled on Poker Life; payment is handled by Stripe on Stripe’s own pages. Eligible options may include cards, Apple Pay, and Link. This website does not collect or store card details.

Can I get a refund or exchange?

For Ripple preorders, see the preorder cancellation policy. For other purchases, the terms presented at checkout apply; email Larry if you need clarification before ordering.

For a specific order, get in touch through the contact page and it can be looked at directly.

Are the books sold anywhere else, like Amazon?

Each book’s details and direct-checkout pages include an optional Amazon search link as an alternative. Poker Life direct checkout remains the primary option; availability, pricing, and delivery on other retailers are controlled by those retailers.

Do you offer bundles or discounts on multiple books?

Use the shared cart to collect more than one book. The cart creates one secure Stripe checkout for all selected titles.

Learning & the author

Books, Articles, Coaching

Which book should I start with?

It depends on the games you actually play. The catalogue spans strategy, tournament play, poker math, reading opponents, and the mental side of the game, and each card carries a one-line summary of its angle.

The articles page is a free way to see the approach before buying. For a recommendation, describe your games on the contact page.

Who writes these books?

Larry McCracken, who has spent more than 25 years in poker as a player, dealer, poker room manager, and author. There is more on the About Larry page.

Will these books make me a winning player?

No book can promise that, and this one will not pretend otherwise. Poker involves real money and significant short-term luck, and results over any small sample say more about variance than about skill.

What the books aim to do is improve the quality of your decisions, which is the part of the game you control.

Do you offer coaching or courses?

They are listed as coming soon, which means there is nothing to book or buy at the moment — no sessions, no prices, no waitlist system behind the scenes. You can email Larry with questions or interest; an email does not reserve a place.

Do the articles cost anything?

No. Everything on the articles page is free to read, with no sign-up.

Newsletter, Forms & Contact

I signed up for the newsletter — did it work?

No. The newsletter form is not connected to a mailing list yet. When you submit it, the confirmation you see is generated in your own browser and nothing is sent, stored, or subscribed. The form is in place ready for a mailing service to be connected later.

How do I send Larry a message?

Use the Email Larry button to open your email app, then write and send your message there. If you use webmail, copy the address from the contact page into a new email. Clicking the button does not send anything automatically.

What is Larry’s email address?

Email larrymccrackenjr@gmail.com for books, Ripple preorders, delivery questions, cancellations, or general enquiries. For an existing order, include your order reference and the email used at checkout.
